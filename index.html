I’m a Research Intern at the AI Institute of South Carolina, advised by Dr. Amit Sheth & Vishal Pallagani. Previously, I interned at EY where I worked on Agentic frameworks and implementation.

My research interests lie in creating trustworthy and reliable large language models by combining behavioral analysis with intervention design.
